# Project brief: WellPath
## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: Entry date and submission date. For the entry you need to submit a pdf, up to 10 pages and a title page. For the submission you need to submit a video explaining and showing your website
## The problem
- People in the word struggle with being healthy because they can't get the motivation to eat a healthy diet and exercise.
- Impact area: Health and wellbeing
- Why it matters (impact): If my project works, these people's lives will become more exciting because of their healthy body and calm mindset.
- Evidence it’s real: About 500 million people get cardiovascular diseases from being inactive. https://www.who.int/news/item/26-06-2024-nearly-1.8-billion-adults-at-risk-of-disease-from-not-doing-enough-physical-activity/

- What people told me: Person A: " I had no energy and didn't want to do anything."

Person B: " At school, I almost collapsed when running the mile."

## My solution

- My AI’s job: My AI helps by motivating you to stay active and keeping track of what you eat, which works better than managing yourself because it remembers what you ate and motivates you to stay active.

- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: MyFitnessPal is a tool that already exist, it records meals and exercises. My tool can make it different by making it student-friendly and give encouraging words. It can also be different by planning a schedule for workouts and meals.

- Safety plan: Risk: Users exercise too hard and get seriously injured.

Plan: the AI can ask if they feel faint and very weak and if the user says yes. The AI tells them to take a break and asks if they need medical service.


Risk: Collecting personal information from the user. 

Plan: the AI will ask if the user is under 13, if yes parents need to agree to pass on personal information.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)

1. The user can log a meal and the AI replies with encouraging, student-friendly feedback.

2. The user can log exercise and see their active minutes for the day.

3. The user can get an AI-made weekly plan for workouts and meals.

4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.

## My AI’s instructions

- When it answers: The user types something and clicks enter.

- Its job: My AI's job is to give reminders, diets, feedback, and keep track of what you eat and your movement.

- Who it talks to and how to sound: Anyone, it should sound formal in gentle way.

- Facts it must use: Facts my AI use is that it must ask if your okay in while. My AI also can

t use  language, titles, or design that imply they are licensed medical professionals.

- Rules it must follow: My AI can never ask for personal information for people under 13.

- How to answer: My AI should answer in a few sentences and first it shows what to eat and exercise and why.
